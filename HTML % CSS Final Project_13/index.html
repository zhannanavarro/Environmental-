What should we do to prevent those problem?

To prevent environmental problems, you must adopt sustainable habits like reducing single-use plastics, recycling diligently, and conserving energy and water at home. Making conscious transportation choices—such as walking, cycling, or using public transit—significantly lowers carbon emissions while buying local, eco-friendly products reduces your overall carbon footprint. Additionally, protecting native ecosystems by planting trees, avoiding harmful garden chemicals, and minimizing meat consumption collectively helps preserve natural resources and ensures a healthier planet for future generations
